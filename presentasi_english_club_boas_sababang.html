PART 01 • RESEARCH TOPIC

Should Elementary School Teachers Be Able to Speak English Fluently?

Yes! For 3 Main Reasons
REASON 01

Accurate Foundation

Children imitate their teachers easily. Accurate teacher pronunciation builds a strong foundation early on.

Real Classroom Example:

If a teacher pronounces daily instructions like "Line up, please" correctly, students will naturally build good pronunciation from a young age.

REASON 02

Global Learning Resources

Teachers can directly access and adapt rich international teaching materials without language barriers.

Real Classroom Example:

A teacher can use interactive science videos or educational games from foreign websites to make classes more engaging.

Interactive Science Videos & Global Games
REASON 03

Habit Building

Consistent daily exposure removes the psychological fear of learning a foreign language.

Real Classroom Example:

Greeting students in English every morning makes them treat English as a normal communication tool, not a scary exam subject.
